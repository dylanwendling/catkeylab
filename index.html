Free Online Keyboard & Mouse Testing Tools

CatKeyLab is a free collection of browser-based tools designed to help you test, troubleshoot, and measure the performance of your computer input devices. If you want to verify that every key on your keyboard registers correctly, check that all your mouse buttons are working, measure your typing speed in words per minute, or test your clicking speed and reaction time, CatKeyLab provides focused tools for each task.

All tests run directly in your browser with no downloads, installations, or account creation required. Your keystrokes, clicks, and test results are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server. CatKeyLab also includes cognitive benchmarks for sequence memory, number memory, verbal memory, visual memory, and reaction time: giving you everything you need to test your gear and your own skills.

🖥️ How to Test Your Keyboard

Use the Keyboard Tester to verify that each key on your physical keyboard is registering correctly:

  1. Open the Keyboard Tester and click inside the test area.
  2. Press each key on your physical keyboard one at a time.
  3. Confirm that each key highlights on the on-screen layout.
  4. Look for keys that fail to register or show incorrect key codes.
  5. Test modifier combinations (Shift, Ctrl, Alt) and try pressing multiple keys simultaneously to check rollover.

People usually test keyboards when checking a new purchase, evaluating a used keyboard before buying, troubleshooting an unresponsive or stuck key, verifying a laptop keyboard, or confirming that a mechanical keyboard's switches are all functioning after cleaning or modification.

🖱️ How to Test a Mouse

Use the Mouse Button & Movement Tester to check that your mouse buttons, scroll wheel, and cursor tracking are working properly:

  1. Open the Mouse Tester and click inside the test area.
  2. Press each mouse button: left, right, middle (scroll click), and side buttons if available.
  3. Scroll the wheel up and down to confirm scroll detection.
  4. Move the mouse to verify smooth cursor tracking.
  5. Use the Double Click Tester to check for unintended double-click chatter.

If your mouse is acting up, check for buttons that don't work, buttons that trigger without being pressed (indicating switch chatter), scroll wheel directions that do not detect, or jerky cursor movement. If a single click is registering as a double-click, your mouse switch may be worn and should be tested with the Double Click Tester.

Why Test Your Keyboard or Mouse?

Testing your devices can save you a lot of headaches:

  • New device verification: Confirm that every button and key works correctly out of the box before your return window closes.
  • Used or refurbished purchases: Test a used keyboard or mouse before committing to a purchase to detect worn switches or broken keys.
  • Troubleshooting input problems: If a key is not responding or a mouse button behaves inconsistently, testing helps isolate whether the issue is hardware or software.
  • Double-click issues: Aging mouse switches can develop "chatter," causing accidental double-clicks. The Double Click Tester measures click intervals to detect this.
  • Performance measurement: Track your typing speed, clicking speed, and reaction time to monitor improvement over time.
  • Keyboard rollover verification: Gamers can test whether their keyboard supports pressing multiple keys simultaneously using the Keyboard Tester.

Everything You Need For Clicking & Hardware Diagnostics: Directly In Your Browser

CatKeyLab is a free website where you can test your mouse and keyboard without installing anything. It provides diagnostic tools for keyboard keys, mouse buttons and scroll wheel, double-click behavior, and typing speed, along with performance benchmarks for clicking speed, reaction time, and aim accuracy.

Everything runs right in your browser, so it works perfectly on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, iOS, and Android with no installation or administrator permissions. All tests happen on your own device: we never record or send your keystrokes or clicks anywhere.

What you can find out: Whether keys and buttons register correctly, which key codes your keyboard sends, whether your mouse has double-click chatter, your typing speed and accuracy, your clicking rate, and your visual reaction time. What these tests can't fix: CatKeyLab cannot access hardware internals, diagnose electrical faults, or detect issues that do not produce observable browser-level events. If a key or button does not register in the tester, the problem could be the switch, wiring, driver, or OS-level configuration.

If you're having issues, try the Keyboard Tester or Mouse Tester to check whether your device is sending events to the browser. If a key or button is not detected, try a different USB port, check your device drivers, or test in another browser to determine whether the issue is hardware or software.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is CatKeyLab completely free to use?

Yes, 100% free with no subscriptions, accounts, or software downloads required.

Does CatKeyLab record my keystrokes or mouse clicks?

No. All input events are processed entirely inside your local browser sandbox and are never transmitted to any external server.

How do I test if my mouse is double-clicking accidentally?

Use the Double Click Tester. It measures millisecond intervals between consecutive clicks; switches releasing bounce signals below 80ms are flagged as switch chatter.

What is a good typing speed (WPM)?

The global average typing speed is roughly 40 WPM. Speeds between 60-80 WPM are considered fast, and 90+ WPM places you in the top 5% of typists.

What is the average human reaction time?

The median human reaction time for visual stimuli is approximately 250 milliseconds. Dedicated gamers typically score between 180ms and 220ms.
