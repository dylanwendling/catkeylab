在线自动点击器

Free online auto clicker running directly in your browser. Set click intervals, target counts, and hotkeys with zero software downloads.

How to Use 在线自动点击器

  1. Set your desired click interval (time between clicks in milliseconds).
  2. Optionally set a target click count, or leave unlimited for continuous clicking.
  3. Click the "Start" button or press the configured hotkey to begin auto-clicking.
  4. The auto clicker will click at the specified interval within the browser page.
  5. Click "Stop" or press the hotkey again to stop auto-clicking.

What This Test Measures

This is a utility tool rather than a test. It generates automated click events at a configurable rate within the browser page. The click counter tracks total automated clicks performed.

Why Use This Test

Online auto clickers are useful for browser-based games that require repetitive clicking, testing click-handling code, or automating repetitive in-page interactions without installing desktop software.

How to Interpret Your Results

The counter displays total clicks performed. The actual click rate may vary slightly from the configured interval depending on browser timer precision and system load.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Start with a moderate interval (100ms+) to avoid overwhelming your browser.
  • Use the hotkey feature for convenient start/stop control.
  • This tool only clicks within the browser page: it cannot click outside the browser window.
  • If you need very high click rates, note that browser timers have a minimum resolution of approximately 4ms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an online auto clicker work?

An online auto clicker uses browser JavaScript timers to simulate rapid mouse click events automatically inside the active web page canvas.

Can an online auto clicker click outside the browser?

No. Due to browser security sandbox rules, web applications cannot control the mouse cursor on your Windows or Mac desktop outside the browser window.

Is this auto clicker 100% free to use?

Yes! CatKeyLab is 100% free, private, and requires zero installation or account creation.

What is the fastest click interval I can set?

You can set intervals as low as 1ms, but browser JavaScript timers have a minimum effective resolution of approximately 4ms. Very fast intervals may not execute at the exact specified rate.
