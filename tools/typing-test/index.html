Typing Speed Test (WPM)

Distraction-free, Monkeytype-inspired typing speed test. Test your WPM and accuracy live with mechanical sounds and Eye Mascot judging.

How to Use Typing Speed Test (WPM)

  1. Select a test duration (15s, 30s, or 60s).
  2. Start typing the displayed text: the timer begins with your first keystroke.
  3. Correct characters are highlighted green; mistakes are highlighted red.
  4. Your live WPM and accuracy update in real-time as you type.
  5. After the timer ends, your final WPM, accuracy, and character counts are displayed.

What This Test Measures

This test tracks your typing speed (WPM: Words Per Minute) and accuracy percentage. WPM is calculated using the standard measurement where one "word" equals 5 characters, including spaces. Accuracy is the percentage of correct keystrokes out of total keystrokes.

Why Use This Test

Knowing your typing speed is useful for job applications that require a minimum WPM, academic assessments, personal improvement tracking, or simply curiosity. Regular practice with typing tests can measurably improve your speed and accuracy over time.

How to Interpret Your Results

Under 30 WPM is considered slow. 30–50 WPM is average for casual typists. 50–80 WPM is above average and typical for office workers. 80–120 WPM is fast. 120+ WPM is professional or competitive level. Accuracy above 95% is considered good.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Focus on accuracy first: speed comes naturally with correct technique.
  • Maintain proper finger positioning on the home row (ASDF / JKL;).
  • Do not look at the keyboard while typing.
  • Take the test multiple times and track your improvement over time.
  • Use the longer test durations (30s or 60s) for more accurate WPM measurements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is typing speed (WPM) calculated?

WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated as (Standardized Words Typed / Minutes Elapsed), where 1 standardized word equals 5 characters.

What is a good typing speed?

Average typists score around 40 WPM. Office workers typically type 50–80 WPM. Professional typists and competitive speed typists often exceed 100 WPM.

Does CatKeyLab store what I type?

No. All typing data is processed locally in your browser. No keystrokes, text, or typing patterns are transmitted to any server.

How does the Eye Mascot judge typing speed?

The mascot calculates your final WPM and accuracy percentage, reacting with expressions and funny judging dialogue based on your performance level.

Can I test my typing speed on a phone?

Yes, though mobile soft keyboards (like Gboard) produce different WPM results than physical keyboards due to auto-correction, swipe typing, and different key layouts.
