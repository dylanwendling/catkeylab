🐾 Real Life Inspiration

Meet Nibbles in Real Life! 🐱

This adorable orange cat sitting inside a cozy cardboard box is the real-life inspiration behind Nibbles! Created by Dylan, Nibbles lives right here on CatKeyLab to keep you company while you test hardware switches, diagnose mouse chatter, practice fast typing, and play companion arcade games!

🎮 Discover Dylan's Creations on itch.io ↗ ⛳ Play Mini Golf with Nibbles →

✨ What Can Nibbles Do on CatKeyLab?

👀

Pupil & Cursor Tracking

Nibbles' emerald eyes dynamically follow your mouse cursor across the screen in real-time as you navigate tools.

🐾

Swatting Paws & Petting

Move your cursor close to Nibbles to see his white paws reach out to swat! Click Nibbles directly to pet him and hear him purr.

⌨️

Typing Speed Judging

During typing speed tests, Nibbles watches your accuracy. Type fast to make him purr happily, or watch him squint at typos!

🧶

Throwable Physics Yarn Ball

Click the yarn toggle to drop a bouncy physics yarn ball that bounces off screen edges with drag-and-throw momentum.

🥣

Interactive Fish Feeding

Click the cat food bowl in the mascot widget to toss fresh fish 🐟 to Nibbles and watch him celebrate.

⛳

Companion Arcade Games

Join Nibbles in 5 companion mini-games: Mini Golf, Cat Fishing, Fruit Slicer, Fish Maze, and 3D Card Memory.
