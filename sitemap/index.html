🛠️ Hardware Testing & Benchmark Tools

  • ⏱️ Reaction Time Test - Check your reaction time. Wait for the signal to turn green and click as fast as you can.
  • 🧠 Sequence Memory Test - Remember an increasingly long pattern of button presses with Simon Says interactive tones.
  • 🎯 Aim Trainer - How quickly can you hit 30 targets? Test your mouse precision and reflex speed.
  • 🔢 Number Memory Test - Remember the longest number sequence you can. The number length increases every level.
  • 💬 Verbal Memory Test - Keep as many words in short term memory as possible. Identify whether each word is SEEN or NEW.
  • 🐒 Chimp Test - Are you smarter than a chimpanzee? Memorize number locations before they turn into blank tiles.
  • 🔳 Visual Memory Test - Remember an increasingly large board of squares as the grid expands.
  • ⌨️ Typing Speed Test (WPM) - Distraction-free, Monkeytype-inspired typing speed test. Test your WPM and accuracy live with mechanical sounds and Eye Mascot judging.
  • 🖱️ Mouse Tester - Interactive online mouse button and scroll wheel tester. Test Left, Right, Middle, Side Back/Forward buttons and cursor tracking.
  • 🖥️ Keyboard Tester - Interactive visual keyboard key tester. Press any key to see real-time highlight feedback, event keycode logging, and modifier status.
  • 👆 Double Click Tester - Test your double-clicking speed and detect faulty mouse switch hardware chatter with millisecond precision.
  • ⚡ CPS Test (Clicks Per Second) - Test your Clicks Per Second (CPS) with our free online CPS calculator. Select 1s, 5s, 10s, 30s, or 60s tests and track your personal best.
  • 🚀 Click Speed Test - Measure your click velocity, burst speed, and clicking consistency with real-time analytics and dynamic speed gauges.
  • 🔢 Digital Click Counter - Online digital click counter with audio sound effects, touch vibration, keyboard shortcuts (Spacebar), and target goal tracking.
  • 🎯 Online Auto Clicker - Free online auto clicker running directly in your browser. Set click intervals, target counts, and hotkeys with zero software downloads.
  • ⛳ Nibbles Mini Golf (18 Holes) - HTML5 Canvas 2D Physics Mini Golf with 18 holes, dynamic weather, wind, strategic sand traps, water hazards, kinetic windmills, and portals!
  • 🎣 Cat Fishing Game - Interactive 2D Cartoon Fishing Adventure with Nibbles the Cat 🐱! Cast your line, click & drag your lure underwater to attract hungry fish 🐟, and mash REEL to catch!
  • 🍉 Fruit Slicer Arcade - Juicy 2D Fruit Slicer Arcade with Nibbles 🐱! Drag or swipe your blade to slice flying fruits ⚔️, trigger multi-slice combo multipliers, and defend your 3 lives!
  • 🐟 Nibbles Fish Maze - Help Nibbles 🐱 navigate through a dynamic maze to find and eat the delicious Fish 🐟!
  • 🎴 Card Memory Match (3D) - Flip 3D cat-themed cards to find all 8 matching pairs in the fewest turns possible.
