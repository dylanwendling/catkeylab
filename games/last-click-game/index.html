💣 Last Click

Step into Last Click: a tactical arcade grid game for CatKeyLab. Uncover numbered tiles, interpret spatial logic, and collect board-based tactical power-ups. Disarm suspected mines with precision Defusers, scan dangerous quadrants with Scanners, survive accidental detonations with Shields, and reveal safe pockets with Sonar Pings.

💣

Last Click

Tactical grid power-up game featuring board-based special tiles, interactive mine defusal, shield barriers, combo streaks, and daily seeded challenges.

How to Use Last Click

  1. Left Click or Tap hidden tiles to reveal them. Your very first click is 100% guaranteed safe with an opening cascade.
  2. Numbers indicate exactly how many mines touch that specific tile (horizontally, vertically, and diagonally).
  3. Right Click or toggle Flag Mode on mobile to mark suspected mine locations with red flags.
  4. Discover and collect special tiles directly on the board: Scanners (3x3 radar), Defusers (safely neutralize mines), Shields (absorb detonations), Reveal Pulses (open safe zones), Sonar Pings (reveal 3 safe tiles), Double Points, and Chain Reactions.
  5. Target power-ups by selecting them in your inventory, then clicking a target board tile.
  6. Use Chording: Click an already-revealed numbered tile whose adjacent flag count matches its clue to instantly reveal all unflagged neighbors!
  7. Clear all safe non-mine tiles across Easy (6x6), Medium (8x8), Hard (11x11), and Impossible (14x14) boards to achieve victory!

What This Test Measures

Last Click evaluates spatial deductive reasoning, probabilistic risk assessment, tactical resource allocation, and cognitive composure under uncertainty.

Why Use This Test

Unlike traditional grid deduction where 50/50 guesses can abruptly end a long run, Last Click equips players with tactical power-ups and protective shields to overcome ambiguous boards through strategic resource management.

How to Interpret Your Results

Clearing Easy in under 30s demonstrates swift spatial deduction. Medium victories reflect solid multi-step planning. Conquering Hard, Impossible, or Daily Seeded challenges marks true tactical Grandmaster mastery.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Look for the 1-1 and 1-2-1 edge patterns to identify guaranteed safe and mine tiles without guessing.
  • Save your Defusers for unavoidable 50/50 corner guesses to remove the mine and update surrounding numbers safely.
  • Use Scanners when entering dense minefields to pinpoint high-threat clusters before clicking.
  • Keep your Shield active as insurance against misclicks and rapid chording maneuvers.
  • Play the Daily Challenge mode every day to compete on a globally identical seeded board against players worldwide!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the First-Click Protection work in Last Click?

Your first click on any board is guaranteed to be 100% safe. Mines are procedurally generated only after your first click, guaranteeing that your opening move opens an informative safe cascade.

What happens when I use a Defuser on a mine?

Targeting a suspected mine with a Defuser neutralizes the mine, removes it from the underlying board data, marks the tile as safe, and automatically decrements all adjacent numbered clues by 1!

How does the Shield protect me?

If you accidentally reveal an unflagged mine while carrying a Shield, the shield absorbs the explosion, neutralizes that mine, updates surrounding numbers, and keeps your run alive.

What is the Daily Challenge mode?

The Daily Challenge generates a unique board layout based on the current UTC date. Every player across the globe receives the exact same board and power-up layout for fair daily competition.

Can I play Last Click comfortably on mobile devices?

Yes! Last Click features a mobile-friendly 2-tier HUD, dedicated Flag Mode toggle button, touch-action panning, and responsive scaling that fits phone screens without vertical scrolling.
